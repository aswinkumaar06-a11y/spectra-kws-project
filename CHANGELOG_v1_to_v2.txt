The updated bundle `Spectra_Arena_Review_v2.zip` includes the following evidence clarifications and audit refinements requested by Arena.ai:

1. MANIFEST & SPEAKER IDENTITY CLARIFICATIONS:
   - Split manifests (`train_manifest.json`, `val_manifest.json`, `test_manifest.json`) now distinguish between:
     * Human speaker identity (`anonymous_speaker_id`, anonymized consistently via SHA256 across all splits).
     * Original recording identity (`original_recording_identity`).
     * Synthetic voice identity (`synthetic_voice_identity`: Microsoft David Desktop vs. Microsoft Zira Desktop).
     * Synthetic speaking-rate settings (`synthetic_speaking_rate_wpm`: 130, 160, 190, 220).
     * Augmentation derivative type (`clean_raw` vs. `noise_or_shift`).
   - `dataset_summary.json` explicitly states that Microsoft Zira Desktop appeared in training at 130/160 WPM, and the 24 synthetic confounder test clips evaluate held-out speaking-rate settings (190/220 WPM) of that known voice, rather than an unseen synthetic speaker.

2. TERMINOLOGY ALIGNMENT IN AUDIT DOCUMENTS:
   - Changed "verified fixed" to "reported locally tested" for live audio buffering and threshold sweeps until verified by Arena.ai.
   - Clarified "0 false-positive classifications among 24 synthetic confounder clips" rather than claiming a zero continuous false-activation rate across streaming audio.
   - Labeled the 10-second live buffering test as a "short streaming smoke test" rather than long-duration validation.
   - Explicitly distinguished processing time per 500ms hop (4.91 ms avg) from end-to-end wake-word detection latency.
   - Distinguished classifier-only evaluation on isolated clips from real-time energy-gated streaming.
   - Clarified that class weighting is implemented and active in training, but does not mathematically prove elimination of all bias.
